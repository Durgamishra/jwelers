Our Heritage

A Legacy of Trust

We are one of the oldest business families in India with a family legacy of over 100 years in business. Started for the noble cause of nation-building and self-sustenance in a pre-independent India, our forefathers believed ethical, honest and transparent business practices should form the foundation of the group.

Having ventured into jewellery retailing in 1993 in Thrissur, Kerala, we have grown to over 140 showrooms spread across India and GCC, bringing the finest craftsmanship from every corner of India to your doorstep.
